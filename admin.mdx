The dashboard is where your organization is set up. Prescribers don't prescribe here: they use [Prescribe](/prescribe/overview).

| Page | Use it to | Who | Open in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Credentials](/admin/credentials) | Create API credentials and webhooks | Developers | [Neutron](https://app.neutron.health/settings/developers) · [Photon](https://app.photon.health/settings/developers) |
| [Team](/admin/team) | Invite people and choose their roles | Administrators | [Neutron](https://app.neutron.health/settings/team) · [Photon](https://app.photon.health/settings/team) |
| [Catalog](/admin/catalog) | Choose the treatments your organization prescribes | Administrators | [Neutron](https://app.neutron.health/settings/catalog) · [Photon](https://app.photon.health/settings/catalog) |
| [Templates](/admin/catalog#templates) | Save prescriptions with the drug, sig and dispense filled in | Administrators | [Neutron](https://app.neutron.health/settings/templates) · [Photon](https://app.photon.health/settings/templates) |

Each environment has its own dashboard, team, catalog and templates. Nothing carries over from Neutron to Photon. On [Grow](/use-cases#platforms-and-marketplaces), each organization you run has its own, too.
