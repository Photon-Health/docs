Photon is prescribing infrastructure for AI-enabled care. Your product or agent drafts the prescription. A prescriber reviews, signs and sends it. Photon then texts the patient their pharmacy options with prices, and the patient chooses where to fill it.

## For prescribers: fast, safe, secure

|  | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Fast** | Your backend or an agent can draft the whole order, so the prescriber only reviews, signs and sends. Templates fill in the drug, sig and quantity. Drug search lists each drug by form and strength, and offers its dispense units. |
| **Safe** | Every prescription is screened against the patient's allergies, medications and conditions before it's signed. When Photon isn't sure which patient or drug is meant, it asks instead of guessing. |
| **Secure** | Only a signed-in prescriber can sign, and the signature covers exactly what they reviewed. Machine credentials can draft but never sign. |

## For patients: price transparency and choice

Nothing goes to a pharmacy until the patient picks one. The text Photon sends lists their options with price, hours, delivery and stock. Discount-card and sponsored offers are included where they apply. The patient gets status updates, and if a pharmacy can't fill the order, they switch in one tap. More prescriptions get filled, for less, with less work for your team.
