The Prescribe app is Photon's own web app for prescribers. Use it before you integrate, or next to your product: a [deep link](/prescribe/app/deep-links) from your product opens it on the right patient or order.

## Signing in

Prescribers sign in with their Photon account. They need an invite to your organization with the **Prescriber** role. See [Team](/dashboard#team). Someone in more than one organization picks one when they sign in.

## Prescribing

**Prescribe** in the top bar starts a new order:

1. Find the patient, or add them.
2. Search for a drug and fill in the sig and quantity, or start from a [template](/dashboard#templates). Screening runs as the prescriber types.
3. Sign each prescription.
4. Click **Send**. Photon texts the patient a link to choose a pharmacy.

## The address bar is the link

The URL always points at the work on screen. Copy it to hand the same order to a colleague, and it opens just as it was. Once the order exists, the link opens that order rather than starting a new one.

| Path | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| `/order` | A new order. `/` goes here. |
| `/patient` | Find or add a patient, and edit their details |
| `/prescription` | One prescription on its own |

To open the app on a record from your product, an email or a text, build a [deep link](/prescribe/app/deep-links).
