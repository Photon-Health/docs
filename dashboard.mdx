The dashboard is where your organization is set up. Prescribers don't prescribe here: they use [Prescribe](/prescribe/overview).

| Page | Use it to | Open in |
| --- | --- | --- |

Each environment has its own dashboard, team, catalog and templates. Nothing carries over from Neutron to Photon.

## Developers

**API credentials** are the `client_id` and secret your code uses. Application credentials sign prescribers in to Prescribe. Machine credentials let your backend call the API. See [Authentication](/authentication) for which to use. If a secret leaks, rotate it here.

**Webhooks** tell your backend when prescriptions and orders change. See [Webhooks](/network/webhooks).

## Team

Everyone who uses Photon needs an invite to your organization, including prescribers who only use Prescribe inside your app.

1. Open **Team** and click **Invite user**.
2. Enter their email and choose one or more roles.
3. Click **Send invitation**. They get an email, and they join your organization the first time they sign in.

Choose the least-privileged role that fits each person's work. You can assign more than one role when someone needs capabilities from both.

| Role | Best for | Key permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Organization owners and team administrators | Manage the organization, team, catalog and templates. View orders and prescriptions, and create or edit patients. Cannot sign, send, reroute or resend orders. |
| **Developer** | Engineers integrating Photon | Manage API credentials and webhooks. Cannot access patients, orders or prescriptions. |
| **Prescriber** | Clinicians who prescribe | Create and update patients, create and send orders, and write and sign prescriptions. Can reroute an order. Cannot resend an order unless they also have a role with `reroute:order`. |
| **Medical Operations** | Clinical operations teams | Create and update patients, create and send orders, and manage shared templates. Can reroute and resend orders. Cannot sign prescriptions. |
| **Staff** | Non-clinical organization operators | Manage the organization, team and shared templates. Can reroute and resend orders. Cannot create or edit patients, orders or prescriptions. |
| **Support** | Teams handling patient and fulfillment questions | View patients, prescriptions and orders. Can reroute and resend orders. Cannot create or edit clinical records. |

A prescriber also needs their NPI, phone, fax and address. Pharmacies use them to identify the prescriber, so enter them when you give someone the Prescriber role.

<Note>
  **Reroute Order** is available with `reroute:order`, `write:order`, or `update:order`. **Resend Order** requires `reroute:order`. In the standard roles, Medical Operations, Staff and Support have `reroute:order`; Prescribers can reroute through their order-write permissions.
</Note>

Invites wait under **Pending invitations** until they're accepted. Resend one if the email was missed, or delete it to cancel. Removing someone from the team takes effect immediately.

<Tip>
  Someone who signs in without an accepted invite sees "not associated with any organizations". Send them an invite, or ask them to accept the one in their email.
</Tip>

## Catalog

The catalog is the list of treatments your organization prescribes. Drug search marks treatments that are in your catalog, and prescribers can still choose one that isn't.

To add one, open **Catalog**, click **Add treatment to catalog** and search for it. Removing a treatment can't be undone, but you can add it again.

Templates are built from catalog treatments, so add a treatment to the catalog before you make a template for it.

## Templates

A template is a saved prescription with the drug, sig and dispense filled in, so prescribers don't type the same prescription twice.

- **Organization templates** are shared by everyone in your organization. Admins create them under **Templates** from a treatment in the catalog.
- **Personal templates** belong to one prescriber, who saves them while prescribing.

Each template has an id (`tmp_…`). Pass it as `templateId` to draft a prescription from it. See [Prescriptions](/network/prescription) and [One-click orders](/integrations/one-click-orders).
